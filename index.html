Get help

To report a problem, ask a question or suggest a feature, open a support request. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

How do I add books? Tap + in the Library to import EPUB or text files from Files, share a file or web page to Lector from another app, or browse free public-domain libraries from the Library.

Where are my books and flashcards stored? On your device. If you use iCloud Drive, Lector keeps a copy in your own iCloud so your other devices stay in step.

How do I sync with my Kobo or e-reader? Open Settings, then E-reader sync, and sign in to the same KOReader-compatible sync server your e-reader uses.

Why don't Translate or Explain work? Sentence translation uses Apple's Translation on your device. Explain uses Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.
