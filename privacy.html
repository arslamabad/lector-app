Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Lector is built so that your reading stays yours. The developer of Lector does not collect, store, sell or share any information about you.

What stays on your device

Your books, reading progress, highlights, flashcards, study history and settings are stored on your iPhone or iPad. Dictionary lookups use dictionaries built into the app and work offline.

Apple services

  • iCloud. If iCloud Drive is on, Lector saves a copy of your library and progress in your own iCloud account so your devices stay in step. That data is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer cannot see it.
  • Translation and Explain. Sentence translation uses Apple's Translation framework and Explain uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Both run on your device.
  • Speech. Read-aloud uses the voices built into iOS.

Services you choose to use

  • Reading sync. E-reader sync is off until you turn it on. If you sign in to a KOReader-compatible sync server (for example to keep your place with a Kobo or other e-reader), Lector sends that server your username, a hash of your password, an anonymous fingerprint of each book you sync, your position in it, and a random device ID. That server is run by a third party you pick, under its own terms. You can sign out at any time.
  • Downloads. When you download a book or import a web page, Lector connects directly to the site you chose to fetch it.

What Lector does not do

No accounts with the developer, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Children

Lector does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the Lector support page.
